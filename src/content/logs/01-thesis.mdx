<h2 id="overview">System Overview</h2>

Balancing a unicycle robot is fundamentally an inverted pendulum problem. To maintain stability, we rely on a proportional-integral-derivative (PID) controller that processes IMU data to calculate wheel torque in real-time. This project demonstrates the "system-level integration" of mechanical design and control theory[cite: 5, 30].

<h2 id="control">Control Logic</h2>

The challenge with an unstable system is managing the overshoot and settling time. Our primary objective was tuning the $K_p$, $K_i$, and $K_d$ gains to ensure the Kalman filter convergence matched our physical motor response[cite: 32].
